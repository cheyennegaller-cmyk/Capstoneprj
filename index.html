Japanese Animation

Anime is a style of animation originating in Japan that features distinct visual elements like expressive characters and vibrant art. Outside of Japan, the term specifically refers to Japanese animated films and television series, which cover a wide range of genres for both children and adults.

Wikipedia →

How anime became Popular

Anime grew from a local Japanese industry into a worldwide phenomenon through early television imports, dedicated afternoon cartoons, and the rise of digital streaming platforms.
